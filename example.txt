Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. AI can learn from data, recognize patterns, understand language, solve problems, and make decisions. Common examples include chatbots, voice assistants, recommendation systems, self-driving technology, and image recognition. Machine learning and deep learning are important areas of AI that help systems improve their performance using data. AI is widely used in healthcare, education, finance, agriculture, transportation, and entertainment. It can automate repetitive tasks, improve accuracy, save time, and help people make better decisions.
